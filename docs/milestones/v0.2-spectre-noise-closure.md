# Milestone v0.2 — Spectre transient-noise closure

**Status:** DRAFT / not started · **Target tag:** `v0.2` · **Owner agent:**
`AGENT_02_ANALOG_EVALUATOR` (see `gcb-spec/ai-agents/`)

## One line

Replace the open-source BSIM4 `.noise` surrogate for comparator noise with a
**commercial transient-noise result on a native FinFET model**, and move the
readout verdict from **CONDITIONAL GO → GO** (or execute the documented
fallback and re-gate).

## Why this is the whole milestone

At `v0.1` all four readout gates pass, but on a surrogate:

| gate | v0.1 result | basis |
|---|---|---|
| comparator input-referred noise ≤ 55 µV | **53 µV** | ngspice `.noise` on a **BSIM4 device gm-matched to ASAP7** (`γ_eff ≈ 1.4`); analytic roll-up cross-check 45.8 µV |
| 3σ `V_os` ≤ 5 mV | 3.2 mV | `A_VT = 2.0 mV·µm` proxy + OOS auto-zero |
| energy ≤ 0.6 fJ/MAC | 0.51 fJ/MAC (comparator, gated) · 0.55 total | SPICE transient supply integral + RTL toggle counts |
| readout worst-corner p05 SNDR ≥ 56 dB @ that noise | 56.72 dB | `ss_readout_mc.py`, 9 ramp-INL corners × 233/300/398 K |

The 53 µV rests on a surrogate carrying a **±16 % model band of 44.5–61.5 µV**,
which **straddles the 55 µV gate**. Everything else about the readout is frozen:
RTL is bit-exact (10 000/10 000), the ramp is characterised across 9 PVT
corners, ADR-005 is locked. This milestone closes the one number that is not
silicon-grade.

Reference: `gcb-papers/data/readout/spice/SPICE_RESULTS_V3.md` §7 (surrogate),
§9 (verdict + band), §10 (p05 sensitivity), §11 (fallback);
`gcb-spec/docs/adr/ADR-005-Single-Slope-Readout.md` ("Consequences").

## The single deliverable

A **transient-noise histogram of the comparator decision** at the ramp
crossing, on a simulator with a working FinFET noise model — **Cadence Spectre**
with native BSIM-CMG (acceptable alternatives: Siemens AFS, or a `.noise`- and
`trnoise`-correct OSDI ngspice build), producing **σ of the input-referred trip
point**.

Fixed conditions (the `v0.1` design point — do not re-tune here):

| parameter | value |
|---|---|
| topology | 2-stage chopped + auto-zeroed resistor-loaded preamp + StrongARM latch |
| `I_tail` | 30 µA / stage |
| `C_L` | 0.8 pF |
| `F_chop` | 20 MHz |
| decision window | 20 ns, statically gated on the calibration-known crossing |
| ramp at the crossing | discharge-mode, 4.2 mV/ns |
| supply | 0.8 V |
| PDK | ASAP7 r1p7, BSIM-CMG |

Run matrix:

- ≥ 200 noisy transient runs of a **single** decision per point (noise-seed
  sweep), σ from the trip-point distribution.
- Signal amplitudes `V_sig ∈ {0.05, 0.28, 0.5} · V_FS` (`V_FS = 568 mV`).
- Corners: **TT/27 °C** (primary) plus **SS/125 °C** and **FF/−40 °C** at the
  primary amplitude, to bound the corner spread.
- Report thermal-only and thermal + residual-1/f (post-chop) σ separately.

## Acceptance criteria

The milestone is **met (→ GO)** when **all** of:

1. **Commercial σ ≤ 55 µV** at the design point (TT/27), and the corner spread
   keeps every corner ≤ 55 µV.
2. **MC re-run passes:** `ss_readout_mc.py --set v_comp_noise=<measured σ>
   --pvt --n-mc 200 --calib 3pt`, discharge-mode, against **all 9 ramp-INL
   corner CSVs**, worst-corner **p05 ≥ 56 dB** (new `mc_v2/*_spiceNN_c2.json`).
3. **Cross-check:** the commercial σ is **within ±20 % of the 53 µV surrogate**
   (this validates the open-tool flow, not just supersedes it). A large
   disagreement is itself a finding and blocks the tag until explained.

Contingencies:

- **55 µV < σ ≤ ~61.5 µV** (inside the old surrogate band): readout p05 does
  not cross 56 dB until ≈ 72 µV (`SPICE_RESULTS_V3.md` §10, linear fit
  `p05 ≈ 58.9 − 0.040·µV`), so criterion 2 can still pass. Outcome is a
  **GO with reduced margin**, documented, with the margin number stated
  explicitly.
- **σ > ~62 µV:** invoke the **gm-C integrating first-stage fallback**
  (`SPICE_RESULTS_V3.md` §11) — the input pair drives a capacitor over the
  gated window so gain = `g_m·t_g/C_int,pre`, independent of the 0.8 V load
  headroom, and the noise/current inversion disappears. **First-stage load
  only — no architecture change, no RTL change, no register change.** Re-run
  the whole gate on the new front stage; still ships as `v0.2`.

## Out of scope for v0.2 (parked open items)

- Optical combiner-loss regime — discrete-component bench question.
- 4:1 vs 8:1 first reduction cell.
- Physical closure of the full 2-stage preamp gain and CMFB in a complete
  schematic (the `v0.1` roll-up assumes `G_tot ≈ G1²` from the measured
  single-stage gain 5.3–8.9).
- Any RTL, register-map, ISA, or architecture change. If the gm-C fallback is
  taken it touches the comparator **first-stage load only**.

## Risks

- **Tool access / licensing** for a BSIM-CMG-capable commercial simulator is
  the critical-path dependency.
- ASAP7 ships HSPICE-format cards; Spectre import or a `lang=spectre` wrapper
  may need per-parameter fixups.
- Transient-noise runtime: 200+ seeds × multi-ns windows × sub-ps steps ×
  several points/corners — budget compute.
- BSIM-CMG's built-in thermal-noise model may not reproduce the `γ = 1.0`
  proxy assumption; the `γ_eff ≈ 1.4` seen in BSIM4 is the thing under test.
- `A_VT` and `K_Fn` remain proxies even after this milestone (ASAP7 ships no
  statistical or flicker model) — `v0.2` closes **thermal + residual 1/f**
  transient noise, not mismatch statistics. State this in the verdict.

## Definition of done

`GCB_STATE_COMPRESSION.txt` reads **GO** (or the fallback was executed and the
four gates re-pass); `v0.2` is tagged and released across the affected repos;
the `ai-agents` state blocks are updated and re-dated.
